A PROPOSAL FOR SOMETHING SPECIALUNIVERSITY OF QUEENSLAND
01 / THE VISIONA union. A coming together.

Many communities.
Sangam

“Sangam” is a Sanskrit-derived word meaning union, or coming together.

For one evening, six South Asian societies at UQ share a table, a dance floor, and a purpose. A celebration of the cultures that connect us, with something meaningful to leave behind.

Our home of South Asia is beautiful, vibrant, and full of possibility. This night is a chance to celebrate it while supporting the communities and causes that need us.

PUNJABI ASSOCIATION
Starting bid$20Illustrative perk · final offering to be confirmed

A hosted experience.
A cause worth bidding for.

Inspired by the familiar date-auction stage format, this is framed as a services and experiences auction: guests bid on a clearly described, society-organised activity and a practical perk, hosted by an executive.

Think a banquet lunch with résumé help, a styling session for your next ball, or something homemade. The personality stays; the lot is the activity and service on offer.

Thoughtfully organised ＋

Participation is fully opt-in, with no pressure on executives or guests. Each lot should spell out the activity, inclusions and boundaries in advance. Group lunches are chaperoned, bookings and contact stay with official society channels, and personal contact details are never shared without consent. Organisers should agree a conduct, withdrawal and complaints process and seek relevant UQU and venue review before offering any lots. A change of name alone does not establish compliance.

The auction is an optional fundraising segment. Ticket proceeds remain the main planned contribution to the cause.

Six societies.
A seat for everyone.

A fair way to come together.

Upfront costs would be shared according to each society’s capacity. Society contributions are reimbursed from gross ticket revenue before net proceeds are donated to the chosen charity.

More reach. More possibility.

Six communities can bring a room to life. The ambition is a venue for around 500 seated guests, with competitive catering and support from our societies’ existing sponsors.

Partners in the evening.

Sponsors could be recognised on the night and on event screens. The next step is to explore venues, agree on a cause, and shape the event together.

02

Legal Governance & Inter-Club Risk Shield

Draft the Inter-Club Collaboration MOU & Deed of IndemnityDraft the formal agreement establishing the Lead Society as the contracting agent, guaranteeing joint loss-sharing, seed capital repayment priority, and holding the signatory personally harmless.
Designate the Contracting EntitySelect an incorporated society (such as UQSLA Inc.) as the head signatory on commercial venue contracts to ensure corporate limited liability protection under Queensland law.
Execute Dual Executive SignaturesCollect formal signatures from both the President and Treasurer across all six participating societies (UQSLA, ISC, UQPA, UQNC, UQTELS, UQGS).
Review with UQU Legal ServiceBook a free 20-minute contract check with UQU Student Advocacy & Legal (Building 21A) to verify the MOU before circulating it for execution.
Submit UQU Off-Campus Event NotificationLodge required risk assessment paperwork with the UQU Clubs & Societies team to maintain compliance with student welfare and affiliation rules.
03

Venue Procurement & Catering Contracts

Contacting venues In progressInquire across shortlisted spaces accommodating 500–650 banquet covers: The Greek Club (650 pax), Brisbane City Hall (800 pax), Rydges South Bank (600 pax), Sofitel Central (580 pax), and BCEC.
Lock In Plated 2-Course Alternate DropRequest quotes specifically for pre-plated alternate-drop mains and desserts (eliminating shared platters to preserve tabletop space for place settings and decor), alongside isolated plating for dietary requirements.
Cap the Bar Turnover LiabilityEnsure venue contracts either enforce a 0 minimum bar spend or set an achievable threshold (≤ $2,000–$4,000) where table soft drinks and water count toward the requirement.
Confirm Operational WindowsConfirm a 2-to-3-hour daytime bump-in access window, a 60-to-90-minute midnight pack-down allowance, and a staffed foyer cloakroom for guest coats and bags.
04

Financial Architecture, Ticketing & Parallel Campaign

Collect Upfront Seed EscrowEnsure each of the six clubs transfers their equal upfront contribution ($2,000–$2,500) into the dedicated Lead Society account prior to paying any venue deposit.
Launch Parallel Direct-to-Charity GoFundMeSet up an independent GoFundMe linked directly to the registered charity to mobilize family, diaspora, and alumni networks—securing the $10,000 donation target externally.
Share the GoFundMe with the communityShare the approved campaign with family, diaspora, alumni, society members and community networks.
Set Accessible Ticket PricingPrice gala tickets at $140–$150 to cover operational overheads, passing the ticketing platform and Stripe merchant fees directly to the buyer on Humanitix.
Build Website Seating & Dietary PortalRequire Humanitix Order ID + Mobile Phone Number for attendee login. Allow Table Captains to register complete tables of 10 or place smaller groups into the general seating pool. Set a hard automated lockout timestamp 14 days prior to the event to finalize numbers for the venue kitchen.
05

Room Styling & Audio-Visual Production

Enforce No-Ceiling & No-Clamp PolicyProhibit suspended ceiling foliage and table-clamping arches to prevent rigging fees, tablecloth pinch damage, and setup delays.
Source Low Botanical Table CenterpiecesDry-hire 50 low-profile potted Boston ferns or artificial Monstera in brass/gold planters with battery-operated warm micro-fairy lights, leaving sightlines and plate space wide open.
Dry-Hire Perimeter LED UplightingReserve 12–16 wireless, battery-powered LED uplights to wash the ballroom perimeter walls in rich emerald green.
Book 1–2 Gobo Leaf ProjectorsHire 1 or 2 Gobo profile light units (around $45 to $55/night each from local suppliers like BJ’s Sound & Lighting) or brief the DJ to use leaf/breakup stencils in their moving-head lights to cast canopy textures onto the ceiling.
Print Foyer Seating Boards at OfficeworksPrint finalized alphabetical seating charts onto mounted A2/A3 foam boards (around $9 to $27 each) and set them on display easels outside the entrance doors.
Secure Arrival Media Wall & Screen AssetsConfirm hire of a freestanding 3m × 2.4m faux green foliage photo wall, and load the animated welcome loop and auction graphics onto the primary AV laptop.
06

The Executive Experience Showcase

Distribute Executive EOI FormCirculate an opt-in questionnaire letting executives choose their offering across three pre-approved tiers: Tier 1 (Tangible Goods & Bribes): Baked Basque cheesecakes, curated DJ playlists, or penultimate-year resume/interview review packages (zero 1-on-1 hangouts required). Tier 2 (Daylight Public Hangouts): Daytime bubble tea runs or Central Library exam cram sessions on campus. Tier 3 (Pooled Group Lunch): A combined reservation for multiple execs at a public table.
Enforce "One Lot = One Standalone Perk"Ensure candidates auction a single clear offering without stacking lunches and goods together.
Execute Executive Opt-In FormsCollect signed consent agreements confirming voluntary participation, chaperone protocols, and absolute right of refusal.
Deploy Digital Bidder Waivers via QR CodePosition a side-stage check-in desk where winning bidders scan and sign the terms agreement before payment confirmation.
Codify the 24-Hour Substitution RuleEnforce strict door refusal for unregistered attendees, permitting transfers only up to 24 hours prior with written notice, signed waivers, and executive veto sign-off.
07

Stage Pacing, Run Sheet & Entertainment

Book Headline DJContract a DJ experienced with South Asian, commercial, and party sets, confirming their equipment footprint and sound patch with the venue.
Enforce the "No Speeches During Dinner" RuleSchedule opening remarks, club acknowledgments, and cultural showcase acts strictly before the first course is served.
Protect the 45-Minute Dining WindowKeep house lights ambient and background music low while servers plate and clear the 2-course meal.
