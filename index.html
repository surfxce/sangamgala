A PROPOSAL FOR SOMETHING SPECIALUNIVERSITY OF QUEENSLAND
01 / THE VISIONA union. A coming together.

Many communities.
One Sangam.

“Sangam” is a Sanskrit-derived word meaning union, or coming together.

For one evening, six South Asian societies at UQ share a table, a dance floor, and a purpose. A celebration of the cultures that connect us, with something meaningful to leave behind.

Our home of South Asia is beautiful, vibrant, and full of possibility. This night is a chance to celebrate it while supporting the communities and causes that need us.

PUNJABI ASSOCIATION
Starting bid$20Illustrative perk · final offering to be confirmed

A hosted experience.
A cause worth bidding for.

Inspired by the familiar date-auction stage format, this is framed as a services and experiences auction: guests bid on a clearly described, society-organised activity and a practical perk, hosted by an executive.

Think a banquet lunch with résumé help, a styling session for your next ball, or something homemade. The personality stays; the lot is the activity and service on offer.

Thoughtfully organised ＋

Participation is fully opt-in, with no pressure on executives or guests. Each lot should spell out the activity, inclusions and boundaries in advance. Group lunches are chaperoned, bookings and contact stay with official society channels, and personal contact details are never shared without consent. Organisers should agree a conduct, withdrawal and complaints process and seek relevant UQU and venue review before offering any lots. A change of name alone does not establish compliance.

The auction is an optional fundraising segment. Ticket proceeds remain the main planned contribution to the cause.

05 / THE COALITIONShared effort. Shared impact.

Six societies.
A seat for everyone.

A fair way to come together.

Upfront costs would be shared according to each society’s capacity. Society contributions are reimbursed from gross ticket revenue before net proceeds are donated to the chosen charity.

More reach. More possibility.

Six communities can bring a room to life. The ambition is a venue for around 500 seated guests, with competitive catering and support from our societies’ existing sponsors.

Partners in the evening.

Sponsors could be recognised on the night and on event screens. The next step is to explore venues, agree on a cause, and shape the event together.
